14 · Conclusions, limitations & next steps

What the completed evidence supports — and what remains open.

14 / 14

Supported conclusions

✓Distance had the strongest observed numerical association with fare among the specified EDA variables.
✓Fare is strongly right-skewed with a meaningful upper tail.
✓Temporal and geographic information added predictive information during controlled validation experiments.
✓E10 was selected under the predefined validation-MAE criterion.
✓Final 2015 H1 test: MAE 1.973790 · RMSE 5.884111 · R² 0.759844.

Limitations / next steps

!Long-distance trips remain a clear weakness.
!Upper-fare tail remains a clear weakness.
!Later-period behavior differs in its error profile; RMSE rises and R² falls from validation to the 2015 H1 test.
→Future work could investigate tail-aware/robust modeling and temporal drift in a separately approved experiment.
→No business ROI, deployment readiness, SHAP, or new model result was measured here.
